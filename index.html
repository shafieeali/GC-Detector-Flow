Detector response vs. carrier flow: FID and TCD

CHM4130 Practice Problem Set 4, Problem 23

The same amount of sample is injected every time. Slide the carrier gas flow and compare how the peak height and peak area change on a mass-sensitive detector (FID) and a concentration-sensitive detector (TCD).

Heights and areas are given relative to the run at flow F. The same mass is injected in every run, and retention time and peak width both scale as 1/F. A mass-sensitive detector responds to the grams per second reaching it, so its peak height follows the flow while its area stays fixed. A concentration-sensitive detector responds to the grams per mL in its cell, so its peak height stays fixed while its area follows the peak’s width in time.
